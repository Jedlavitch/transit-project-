One-time setup — WMATA API key

Train & bus times come from WMATA (Washington Metro). It's free: create an account, then subscribe to the “Default Tier” product and copy your Primary key.

→ developer.wmata.com/signup, then subscribe to Default Tier, then copy your key here.

Stored only in this browser (localStorage). Planes & Amtrak work without it.

MARC & Ride On show scheduled times with no setup (built in) — MARC trains are placed on the map from their schedule.

Aircraft feed (optional)

Planes overhead need a feed you run yourself — this board ships without one, because the free public feeds do not permit commercial use and we will not make that your problem. AIRCRAFT-FEED.md has a ready-made proxy and takes a few minutes on any small server. Leave this empty and the board simply runs without the aircraft card.
