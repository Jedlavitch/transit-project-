Pool your screens

Every board on this device already feeds one list — that is what "All cities" is. A share code pools different devices: your kiosk, your laptop and your phone all write to the same list, and it is also what the daily auto-post reads. Leave it empty and nothing ever leaves this device.

The code is the only access control, and jsonblob.com is a public store — anyone holding it can read your list. That is the same trade the Spotter's share code makes, and it is why nothing is published until you set one.
